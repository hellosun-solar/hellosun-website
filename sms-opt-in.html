SMS Program

SMS Messaging Program

How HelloSun collects your consent, what we send, and how to opt out at any time.

Last updated: June 11, 2026  ·  Operated by HelloSun LLC  ·  Customer portal: HelloStation (my.hellosun.solar)

How you opt in

When you create your HelloStation customer account at my.hellosun.solar/auth/register, you are shown an unchecked SMS consent checkbox during sign-up. You must check it yourself to receive texts. It is never pre-checked, and consent to receive SMS is not required to create an account or to purchase any product or service.

Send me text message updates about my solar project, including appointment and install reminders and status updates. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out anytime.
The exact consent checkbox shown on the HelloStation sign-up form (displayed checked here for illustration; it is unchecked by default).

We record your consent selection and a timestamp at the moment you submit the form. If you do not check the box, you will not receive any SMS from HelloSun.

What we send

Messages are transactional only and relate to your own solar project:

  • Proposal-ready and contract-ready notifications
  • Site survey reminders (appointment time and advisor name)
  • Install date confirmations and pre-install reminders
  • System activation confirmation and service updates

Message frequency: approximately 1 to 4 messages per month during active project phases, and fewer once your system is activated.

Sample messages

HelloSun: Your solar proposal is ready for review. View your system design and pricing at my.hellosun.solar/dashboard. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
HelloSun reminder: Your site survey is scheduled for tomorrow at 10:00 AM. Your advisor will arrive at your home. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
HelloSun: Your solar install is confirmed for Tue Jun 23, 7:30-8:00 AM arrival. View at my.hellosun.solar/dashboard. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Opt out and help

Stop messagesReply STOP to any HelloSun text, or toggle SMS off in your Notification Preferences at my.hellosun.solar.
Get helpReply HELP, email info@hellosun.solar, or visit hellosun.solar/contact.
Carrier ratesMessage and data rates may apply, depending on your wireless plan.
PrivacyMobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for any purpose. See our Privacy Policy, Section 3.
