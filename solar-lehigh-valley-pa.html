Lehigh Valley Solar FAQ

Local questions, straight answers.

How does net metering work with PPL?

The Lehigh Valley is served by PPL, which offers Pennsylvania's 1:1 net metering. Excess solar earns a one-for-one credit that banks month to month, with an annual cash true-up of any surplus.

How long does an install take?

Most projects run 60 to 90 days, mostly PPL and municipal permit review, which we manage. The install itself is usually one to two days.

Do you do commercial solar in the Lehigh Valley?

Yes. We handle residential, commercial, and agricultural projects across the Valley, with real load analysis and interconnection managed from application to approval.
