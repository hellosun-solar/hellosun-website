Additional Services

Support that doesn't end
at installation.

Solar is a decades-long investment, and it deserves decades of support. Beyond design and installation, we keep systems running at their best, add capability as your needs grow, and protect what you've built, whether we installed it or not.

What We Offer

More than panels on a roof.

A short list of the other work we do for homeowners and businesses across our Pennsylvania service area.

System Diagnostics

Underperforming array or a system that stopped reporting? We find the root cause using monitoring data, on-site testing, and a full inspection of your panels, inverter, and wiring, then tell you plainly what's going on.

Troubleshooting & Repair

Failed inverters, faulty optimizers, tripped breakers, loose connections, error codes. We track down the fault and fix it, restoring your system to full production.

EV Charger Installation

Level 2 home and workplace charging installed by licensed electricians, sized to your electrical panel and wired to code, with or without solar. Charge faster and smarter at home.

Critter Guard Installation

Mesh barriers that seal the perimeter of your array to keep squirrels, birds, and other pests from nesting under the panels and chewing wiring, protecting your investment and your roof.

We service systems we didn't install. Inherited a solar system with a house, or working with an installer who's no longer around? Reach out, we're happy to take a look.
